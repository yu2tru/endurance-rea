What is tenant representation?

Tenant representation means a broker negotiates exclusively on the tenant’s behalf to secure the best lease terms. As a tenant advocate, Endurance provides the market data, landlord intel and negotiation strategy tenants need to make informed decisions. Learn more about our tenant representation services.

What is the Tenant Advantage Analysis (TAA)?

The Tenant Advantage Analysis is Endurance’s proprietary, confidential report on a tenant’s specific leasing situation. It covers current rental rates, concessions and comparable properties, intel on the landlord’s motivations and negotiation history, and a customized negotiation strategy.

What happens to my lease if my landlord is in financial trouble?

Landlord distress can give tenants negotiating leverage, such as lower rent, abatements or better renewal terms, but it also brings risks like reduced services and ownership uncertainty. Endurance researches ownership finances, loan performance and debt maturity before negotiating, and helps tenants secure protections such as an SNDA. Read lessons from a landlord’s troubles.

What lease protections should a commercial tenant negotiate?

Commercial tenants in NYC should negotiate a self-help clause, an SNDA and security protections in case of an ownership change. A self-help clause lets you fix certain landlord failures and recover the cost, while an SNDA protects your lease if the lender forecloses. Always have a qualified real estate attorney review your lease.

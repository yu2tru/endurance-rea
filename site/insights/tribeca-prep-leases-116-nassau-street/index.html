Tribeca Prep didn’t choose a newer building. They chose the right building.

  • Tenant: Tribeca Prep
  • Space: 12,900+ SF, entire second floor
  • Location: 116 Nassau Street, between Ann and Beekman Streets, Financial District

Committed ownership in Lower Manhattan

For over three decades, the ownership of 116 Nassau Street has stood resilient, weathering shifting markets, economic recessions, and the constant evolution of Lower Manhattan. That kind of commitment to a building and its tenants is rare, and tenants notice.

Now, 116 Nassau is the proud new home of Tribeca Prep, which has signed for the entire second floor: more than 12,900 square feet of space in the heart of the Financial District, between Ann and Beekman Streets.

Real estate trends come and go, but well-located buildings with committed ownership always endure. More importantly, ownership that understands when to move decisively and capitalize on the right opportunity creates long-term value for both the building and its tenants.

That is the definition of Endurance.

Space still available at 116 Nassau Street

Availabilities remaining at 116 Nassau Street range from 5,231 to 11,365 SF, in multiple configurations. To inquire, contact Greg Gang at greg.gang@endurance-rea.com or 646-662-3546.

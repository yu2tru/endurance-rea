Landlord representation FAQs

What types of properties does Endurance lease for landlords?

Endurance leases buildings, office spaces, retail properties, and condo and coop commercial assets in New York City. We work with property owners, condo and coop boards, and investors in office or retail space.

How do you attract tenants to a property?

We attract tenants by crafting a narrative that showcases your property’s best features and competitive edge, then reaching the right prospects with focused, targeted marketing. Our leasing strategy is built on comprehensive market analysis to achieve optimal pricing and quality tenants.

How involved are you once a prospect is interested?

Very involved. From initial marketing to lease signing, we work closely with ownership to ensure a seamless transition from prospect to tenant, and our skilled negotiation delivers lease terms tailored to your objectives.

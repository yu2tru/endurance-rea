Tenant representation FAQs

What does a tenant representation broker do?

A tenant representation broker negotiates on behalf of the tenant, not the landlord, to secure the best possible lease terms. At Endurance, our sole fiduciary duty is to protect our clients’ interests. We provide the market data, landlord intel and negotiation strategy you need to make informed decisions throughout the process.

What is the Tenant Advantage Analysis?

The Tenant Advantage Analysis (TAA) is Endurance’s proprietary, confidential report on your specific leasing situation. It covers market insights such as rental rates, concessions and comparable properties, intel on your landlord’s motivations and negotiation history, and a customized strategy to secure the best possible lease terms.

Can you help me renew my current lease instead of moving?

Yes. Endurance negotiates renewals and expansions as well as relocations. One client toured over 75 spaces across 9 months before realizing that expanding within their current building made the most financial sense, and Endurance negotiated that new lease. In today’s market, renewing tenants may also benefit from net deals as TI allowances shrink.

When should I start looking for new office space?

Start your search 6 to 18 months before your current lease expires. That window allows time for thorough due diligence, market research and negotiation without pressure, and it gives you leverage if a renewal is also on the table.

What lease protections should NYC tenants ask for?

Key protections include a self-help clause, an SNDA, and security protections in case of an ownership change. These matter more than ever with many NYC landlords under financial pressure. Always have a qualified real estate attorney review your lease.

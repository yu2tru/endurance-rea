Every assignment starts with your goals

We begin by listening. Working closely with you, we establish your short and long-term goals and objectives, whether you need room for a growing company or the right tenants for a building.

Then we do the research. For tenants, that means a confidential Tenant Advantage Analysis covering current rents, concessions, comparable properties and your landlord’s motivations. For owners, it means a leasing strategy built on comprehensive market analysis and a clear story about what makes the property worth leasing.

From there we negotiate, and we stay involved through lease signing. One client toured more than 75 spaces with us over nine months before choosing the option that made the most financial sense. In her words, Endurance was relentless.
